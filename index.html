WHY THIS PLATFORM

Make local work easier to find.

Many community organisations and independent artisans depend on social media, word of mouth or offline networks. This prototype brings essential information into one clear, accessible place.

Find organisations, artisans and their work.

“Good work should not be difficult to discover.”

This website is a student Field Project prototype based on a survey of 113 respondents about NGO and local-artisan website requirements.
